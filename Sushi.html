03 — The counter

Slowed down, on purpose.

We're the small counter at the end of the street — twelve stools, one itamae, no conveyor belt. Fish comes in before sunrise and leaves the same day; rice is seasoned while it's still warm enough to hold. That's the whole trick, and we're stubborn about it.

  1. 01

    Fish first

    Small, daily batches from the harbour. What the market doesn't promise in the morning never reaches the board at noon.

  2. 02

    Rice at body temperature

    Seasoned with red vinegar and kept just warm, so every piece holds together without being squeezed.

  3. 03

    No conveyor, no shortcuts

    Twelve seats means we see every plate we send out. Nothing leaves the counter we wouldn't eat ourselves.

— K. Mori, itamae since 2018
